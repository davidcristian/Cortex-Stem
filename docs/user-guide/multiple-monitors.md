# More than one monitor

With _Monitors_ (Settings →
[Position & monitors](settings-window.md#position--monitors)) set to _All
monitors_, every monitor gets its own dock, centred at the bottom of that
monitor and drawn at that monitor's scale. Stem adds, removes and moves docks
when monitors are plugged in, unplugged or made primary. Each dock magnifies,
auto-hides and reveals on its own. A fullscreen app hides only the dock on
its monitor, and with _When a window overlaps_, a window hides only the dock
it covers. The tray icon and the toggle shortcut show or hide all docks
together. With _Primary monitor only_, the other docks close.

By default, every dock lists every window. Turn on _Show only windows on the
dock's monitor_ to have each dock list only the windows on its own monitor.
Pinned apps stay on every dock. A click on a pinned app on a dock whose
monitor has none of the app's windows activates its window on the other
monitor, instead of starting a second copy.

When you change a display's scale, the dock on it draws its icons at the new
size and stays in its place, without a restart.
